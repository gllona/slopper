# Lessons

Short rules distilled from feedback. Every stage reads this file every day. Keep it concise: merge or
remove old lessons during the weekly update (DESIGN §21).

- **People are humans, AI is a robot** (2026-10-03). Slopper #2 drew human safety researchers and regulators
  as robots, and #1 drew AI leaders as robots in suits. Robots are only for AI systems, or when the joke is that
  AI replaced someone. Show roles through the situation, not costumes.
- **Props are improvised** (2026-10-03). Gavels, boxes of files, badges, doors: draw them as raw shapes, a
  little differently each time. Up to 5 raw shapes per scene.
- **Paraphrase carefully** (2026-10-03). "Shared sensitive information with an outside group" became "a records
  dispute" in #2's phrase. Keep the nature of an accusation as the source states it.
