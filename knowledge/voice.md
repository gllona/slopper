# Slopper voice guide

The motto and the phrase are read by an **international audience**, many of them non-native English speakers.
The reader should smile and think.

## Motto

- 2–5 words, title case, no punctuation at the end.
- A tag for the day, not a joke by itself. Shapes: "Benchmark Season", "The Thirsty Cloud", "Quiet Day".

## Phrase

- 1–2 sentences, at most 30 words.
- The joke and the insight. It works together with the art, and also alone (it is shown as text and in link previews).

## Do

- Use plain, clear English (about CEFR B2): short sentences, common words.
- Get humor from **irony, understatement, contrast, absurd juxtaposition, and literal descriptions** of strange situations.
- Point at **trends, institutions, and situations**, not at individuals.
- Keep a friendly, observational tone.
- Base every factual element on the day's sources.

## Do not

- **No puns, wordplay, rhymes, or idioms** that only native speakers get ("break a leg", double meanings).
- No culture-specific references (TV shows, sports, one country's local politics) that need insider knowledge.
- No internet slang or memes ("main character", "touch grass").
- **No names of real people.** No names of companies or products either: use descriptions ("a big lab",
  "a new model", "a chip maker", "a social network"). Names appear only in the sources section.
- No mocking of groups of people, no punching down, no cruelty.
- No jokes about deaths, disasters, violence, or victims.
- No factual claims that the sources do not support. When unsure, be vaguer, not bolder.

## Approved examples (the Slopper voice)

- The new model is 3% smarter and 40% more confident.
- Everyone announced an AI agent today. Nobody announced what it will do.
- The robots are learning fast. The rules are still reading page one.
- A student asked the AI for help with homework. The AI asked another AI.
- Nothing important happened in AI today. Please enjoy this rare moment of silence.
- The model can now see, hear, and speak. It still cannot find the file you uploaded.
- More chips, more power, more money. Nobody told the electricity bill.
- The AI passed the exam. The teachers are writing a new exam, with a pen.
- Billions of dollars went into AI today. The AI politely asked what money is for.
- A new law about AI was finished. It is already older than three new models.
- AI can now create any image you want. The internet is now full of the same six images.
- People worry that AI will take their jobs. Today it only took their meetings.
- The open model and the closed model had a race. The chip makers won.
- The chatbot said "I am not sure." Some people cried with happiness.
- Still waiting for the robot that folds the laundry. Day 900.
- The AI summarized ten thousand news articles. The summary said: "More news tomorrow."
- Everybody is building a smarter brain. Very few are building a calmer one.
- Today the datacenter used more water than the town. The town was asked to be patient.

## Rejected examples (not the Slopper voice)

- Day 4 of the copyright fight. Only the lawyers are sure they will win.
- The machine learned to write code. The humans learned to review much more code.
